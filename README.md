# 🌿 REVITALIZE

A modern wellness store specializing in premium wellness products and devices designed to transform your health journey.

## About

Revitalize is your destination for carefully curated wellness essentials including:
- Fitness equipment (yoga mats, resistance bands, balance boards)
- Wellness devices (smart fitness watches, air purifiers, light therapy lamps)
- Recovery tools (massage guns, foam rollers, acupressure mats)
- Relaxation accessories (aromatherapy diffusers, meditation cushions)

## Features

- 🛒 **Shopping Cart**: Add products to cart with persistent storage
- 📱 **Responsive Design**: Works seamlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- 🎨 **Modern UI**: Clean, professional design with wellness-focused color scheme
- 💳 **Easy Checkout**: Streamlined checkout process
- 📧 **Contact Form**: Get in touch with our team
- 🔔 **Real-time Notifications**: Instant feedback on cart actions

## Getting Started

Simply open `index.html` in your web browser to view the store.

For development with live reload:
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then navigate to `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.

## Technology Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3 (with CSS Grid and Flexbox)
- Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
- LocalStorage for cart persistence

## Product Categories

- **Wellness Devices**: Smart devices for health tracking and improvement
- **Recovery Devices**: Products for muscle recovery and pain relief
- **Relaxation**: Accessories for mindfulness and stress relief
